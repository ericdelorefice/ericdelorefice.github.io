How To: Exercise Form

Hand Release Push Up

  • Chest, hips, thighs down together
  • Lift both hands off the ground
  • Press to full lockout
  • Body stays in one line

Sit Up

  • Shoulder blades touch the ground
  • Elbows reach the knees
  • Feet held by a partner
  • Controlled tempo

2-Mile Run pacing

  • Run the first lap at goal split, not faster
  • Know your 400 m split
  • Relax shoulders and hands
  • Finish the last 400 m strong

Plank

  • Forearms on the ground
  • Straight line from head to heels
  • Squeeze glutes and brace the core
  • Breathe

Reference: Nutrition, Bloodwork, and Recovery

Macros

For a cadet training 5 days a week, balancing macronutrients is vital. Aim for lean proteins to rebuild muscle, complex carbohydrates to fuel your workouts and maintain energy, and healthy fats to support hormone function and joint health. Note: this is general guidance.

Understanding your bloodwork

Common markers a medical provider may check include:

  • Vitamin D: Crucial for bone health and immune function during heavy training blocks.
  • Iron/Ferritin: Essential for oxygen transport in the blood, heavily impacting cardio endurance.
  • B12: Important for red blood cell formation and nervous system function, preventing fatigue.

Talk to a medical provider before taking any supplement.

Learn more at Human Performance Resources by CHAMP and Operation Supplement Safety.

Recovery

Quality sleep and consistent hydration are just as important as the workout itself. Without proper recovery, performance stagnates.
