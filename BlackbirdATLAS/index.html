Blackbird ATLAS

The 3 Week AI Website Challenge, CSC-1210, Fall 2026

What this was

Blackbird ATLAS is a fitness training site for AFROTC Detachment 605. I wrote one prompt and pasted it unchanged into three different AI tools, so the only thing that changed between the three sites was the AI that built it.

Week 1 was generating the sites. Week 2 was testing them, running both of the W3C validators, taking screenshots in Chrome and Firefox and measuring where things actually landed on the page. Week 3 was writing a correction prompt for each AI and letting it fix its own mistakes.

The three sites below are the corrected versions. All three pass the W3C HTML and CSS checkers with no errors.
