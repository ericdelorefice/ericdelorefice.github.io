Training Plan

Full Training Week
Day Strength Cardio Notes
Monday Det PT "Full Throttle": 40-minute EMOM. Eight exercises, one per minute: hand release push ups, squat jumps, diamond push ups, lunges, sit ups, leg raises, American twists, and plank. Includes a 400 m relay lap rotation. Relay laps count. 0600 Truist Stadium.
Tuesday Upper A: bench press, bent-over row, overhead press, pull-ups, and tricep dips. Easy run, 3 mi. Conversational pace.
Wednesday Lower A: back squat, Romanian deadlift, walking lunges, and calf raises. Tempo run, 3 mi. Last mile at goal PFRA pace.
Thursday Det PT "Final Approach": pace-group descent of 1600 m, 1200 m, 800 m, and 400 m. Each leg is 5 sec per lap faster. Rest is half the run time. 4000 m total. 0600 Truist Stadium.
Friday Upper B: incline dumbbell press, lat pulldown, lateral raises, face pulls, and hand release push ups, 3 x max. 400 m intervals x 6, 90 sec rest. Run in lanes 2-4.
Saturday Lower B: front squat, hip thrust, step-ups, and hamstring curls. Long run, 5 mi. 60-90 sec per mile slower than goal pace.
Sunday Rest. 20-minute walk. Mobility and sleep.

Test week: Preflight taper

For cadets testing next week:

  1. 2 x 800 m at goal 2-mile pace, walk rest.
  2. Push up rehearsal: 30 s / 20 s / 10 perfect reps.
  3. Sit up rehearsal: 30 s / 20 s / 10 perfect reps.
  4. 4 x 100 m strides.
  5. Full cool-down stretch.

Nothing in the taper goes to failure, and no volume is added.

How To: Exercise Form

Hand Release Push Up

  • Keep your chest, hips, and thighs down together.
  • Lift both hands off the ground.
  • Press to full lockout.
  • Keep your body in one line.

Sit Up

  • Make sure your shoulder blades touch the ground.
  • Reach your elbows to your knees.
  • Have your feet held by a partner.
  • Use a controlled tempo.

2-Mile Run Pacing

  • Run the first lap at your goal split, not faster.
  • Know your 400 m split.
  • Relax your shoulders and hands.
  • Finish the last 400 m strong.

Plank

  • Place your forearms on the ground.
  • Keep a straight line from your head to your heels.
  • Squeeze your glutes and brace your core.
  • Breathe.

Reference: Nutrition, Bloodwork, and Recovery

Macros

Protein supports muscle repair, carbohydrates provide fuel for running and lifting, and fats support normal body functions. A cadet training 5 days a week should include all three in regular meals and snacks. This is general guidance, not individualized nutrition advice.

Understanding Your Bloodwork

  • Vitamin D: Supports bone health and normal muscle function.
  • Iron and ferritin: Help support oxygen transport and energy during training.
  • Vitamin B12: Supports red blood cell production and normal energy metabolism.

Talk to a medical provider before taking any supplement.

Recovery

Prioritize consistent sleep, drink enough water throughout the day, and allow your body time to recover between hard sessions.
